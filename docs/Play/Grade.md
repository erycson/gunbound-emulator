## Comandos
brings you to specific room	/go <room number>
Directly send a private message to someone. (spaces between /m, name, and your message)	/m <name> <message>
Displays the people that you have currently muted.	/mutelist
Displays the person's messages if you muted his/her. (spaces between /loud and name)	/loud <name>
Exits the game completely	/exit
help menu	/h
help menu	/help
help menu	/guide
Ignores the person's messages. (spaces between /mute and name)	/mute <name>
Kick anyone from a game. (you MUST be the keyholder of the game, spaces between /kick and name)	/kick <name>
Quit the game and return to your desktop.	/quit
Unmutes all people	/loudall
